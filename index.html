QUICK START

How to fish

  1. Equip the rod from the Equipment tab in the weapon wheel.
  2. Use Focus to spot fish as lighter shapes in the water.
  3. Cast, then reel slowly to attract a bite.
  4. Hook the fish, steer against its movement and reel it in.
  5. Use freshwater lures in lakes and rivers; saltwater lures from docks and at sea.
