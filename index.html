START / EQUIPMENT

Get your first rod

Jason can fish from the beginning of free roam in Chapter 1. A rod and boat are available at the dock behind his home in Key Lento. The starting inventory includes a Small Freshwater Lure and a Small Saltwater Lure.

Freshwater: lakes and rivers. Saltwater: docks, coast and open water. Medium lures and a Large Saltwater Lure are available from selected tackle shops and Ammu-Nation stores.

CONTROLS / CATCH

Land a fish

  1. Equip the Fishing Rod from the Equipment tab of the weapon wheel.
  2. Cast into the hotspot and work the lure until a fish bites.
  3. Set the hook when prompted, steer against the fish and reel it in.
  4. Free-roam fishing is catch-and-release; catches still count in your collection.
CHALLENGE / TROPHY

Cal's fishing challenges

After the early story setup, Cal can mark a fishing challenge on the map. The first challenge asks for a Stoplight Parrotfish weighing more than 2 lb. Beating Cal in a fishing challenge unlocks the Baited My Tackle trophy.

Catch every species for the Pro Angler challenge. Studying a fish and catching it are separate progress actions.

QUICK START

How to fish

  1. Equip the rod from the Equipment tab in the weapon wheel.
  2. Use Focus to spot fish as lighter shapes in the water.
  3. Cast, then reel slowly to attract a bite.
  4. Hook the fish, steer against its movement and reel it in.
  5. Use freshwater lures in lakes and rivers; saltwater lures from docks and at sea.
