PRIVACY POLICY.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This page explains how the VICECITYWIRE website and its installable web app handle information. VICECITYWIRE is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

1. Information stored on your device

VICECITYWIRE uses your browser's local storage to remember selected app preferences and locally managed progress, including checklist entries, collection counts, saved route or trip-planning information, and character or other review states where those features are available. This information is stored in the browser or webview on your device. The site code reviewed for this policy does not send this locally stored progress to a VICECITYWIRE account or VICECITYWIRE server. Clearing the website's storage or app data can remove it. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data.

2. No account or direct submission form

The current website does not provide user accounts, a registration system, or a form for submitting personal information to VICECITYWIRE. The current client-side code reviewed for this policy contains no analytics or advertising tracking integration and does not use a VICECITYWIRE-operated API to transmit checklist progress.

3. Hosting and technical access data

The website is published using GitHub Pages. When you access the site, GitHub and infrastructure providers involved in delivering the website may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, request timestamps, and security or delivery logs. Such processing is governed by the providers' own terms and privacy policies. VICECITYWIRE does not control their infrastructure logs.

4. External links, images and services

VICECITYWIRE links to third-party websites and services, including Rockstar Games, community map providers, game guides, YouTube and other reference sources. Some externally hosted artwork or media may be requested directly from the third-party host when a page is displayed. Opening an external link or loading third-party content can disclose technical connection data, such as your IP address and browser information, to that provider. Third parties may use cookies or other technologies under their own policies. Their practices are not controlled by VICECITYWIRE.

The community map provider may store its own map preferences or discovery progress separately from VICECITYWIRE. Please review the provider's privacy information before using its services.

5. App installation

The installable app is a packaged version of the VICECITYWIRE web experience. It uses local device or webview storage for the same locally managed preferences and progress described above. The app does not currently require an account or request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location.

6. Your choices

  • You can clear site data or browser storage through your browser settings.
  • You can remove the installed app from your device.
  • You can avoid opening external links or loading third-party resources.
  • You can contact VICECITYWIRE with privacy questions using the address below.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or the VICECITYWIRE project, contact vicecitywire1312@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, hosting arrangements or data practices change. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.

Important: This is a project privacy notice based on the current website code and feature set. It is not legal advice. Hosting-provider practices and any future app-store SDKs, crash reporting, analytics, advertising or third-party integrations must be reviewed separately before a public store release.
